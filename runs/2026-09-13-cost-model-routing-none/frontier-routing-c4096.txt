the check: the attention term now has ZERO fitted parameters
  The model used to charge one slope, `alpha`, solved from the two measured
  contexts -- one parameter, two points, one residual dof.  Stream DD's key tile
  model and this module's symbolic re-derivation of it replace that slope with
  a closed form in (S, C) whose every coefficient is an identity of the layout,
  so both measured levels are now predicted with nothing solved: two points,
  zero fitted parameters, TWO residual degrees of freedom.

  the closed form, general in (nh, hd, S, C).  DD froze the coefficients at
  Llama-8B's geometry; these are the same numbers re-derived from the column
  accounting so they can be evaluated at a shape that is not Llama-8B:
      base  = 2*nh*S*C  +  2*nh*hd*C  +  nh*hd*S  +  4*nh*S
      inter = (2 + 24/gt)*nh*S*C  +  (4*nh*hd + 12*nh*hd/ct)*C
              +  2*nh*hd*S  +  (8*nh + 8*nh/rw)*S
  with gt = grid_target (64), ct = chan_target (128), rw = row width (16).
  At nh=32, hd=128 the derived triples are (140.0, 37376.0, 256016.0) fresh and (64.0, 12288.0, 128.0) base.
  Read off `prover/src/attn_trace.rs:1167-1181` with `prover/src/layout.rs`:
    lc.g.rows = hd*insts*C/pk (pk -> ct), lk.g.rows = C*insts*S/q (q -> gt),
    rrows*rw = nh*S, and `insts` sums to nh over the key tiles -- every term is
    linear in `insts`, so grouping the heads 16/8/8 costs exactly what 32 would.

  Three things this derivation does NOT settle, and they are the extrapolation
  risk the frontier rows carry:
   1. **K and V are committed per ATTENTION head, not per KV group.**
      `LayoutChan`/`LayoutKey` are built from `Shape::chans()`/`keys()`, both
      carrying `insts` = attention heads, so the `C` term is 2*nh*hd and `nkv`
      does not appear, and the boundary trees hand on a cache of the same
      width.  At 8B nh/nkv is 4; at Llama-405B and Qwen3 it is 16.  The three
      latent-attention shapes hold no K and no V per key/value head at all --
      what a key costs their cache, both ways, is section (2d).
      What that is worth is now COUNTED and not named: on one 8B layer segment
      the two caches and the boundary trees that hand them on are 7.8% of the
      segment at C=4,096 and 18.4% at C=16,384, and committing them once per key/value
      head instead would leave 5.9% and 13.8% of the segment uncommitted.  Per proved
      position over the whole statement, shape by shape, that is section (2c).
      Not built: no AIR in this project shares a key tile's K and V columns
      between two heads' score grids, and what this table prices is what the
      prover commits today.
   2. **gt / ct / rw are run knobs, not constants.**  The composed sequence used
      64 / 128 / 16 and this model keeps them.  They tile exactly only when S,
      C, hd and `insts` are powers of two and the target divides; `Grid::new`
      also caps q at min(S, hd) and pk at min(C, S).  Where a shape saturates a
      cap the table says so.
   3. **The out (A.V) component is not in the key tile family.**  It is the `attnout`
      family and `cells_per_token` charges it separately, so there is no double
      count -- but it is also the one attention component whose price here is a
      measured per-element constant rather than a derived column count.
  Under the exact count this is not a question the table asks: the grids are
  built rather than closed-form, so a layout that does not tile is counted as it
  tiles and nothing is reported as saturated.
